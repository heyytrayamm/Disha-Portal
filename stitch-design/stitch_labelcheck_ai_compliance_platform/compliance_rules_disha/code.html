tag RULE-MRP-001 open_in_new

Maximum Retail Price (MRP) Declaration

Requirement

MRP must be clearly printed inclusive of all taxes. It cannot be obscured by stickers or additional text.

Why it matters

Ensures consumer protection against overcharging and maintains market transparency per national metrology acts.

rule Verification Method

OCR extraction specifically looking for "MRP", "Rs.", "₹" followed by numeric values. Confidence threshold: > 95%.

tag RULE-MRP-002 open_in_new

Dual MRP Prohibition

Requirement

Multiple MRPs for the same product based on location or channel are strictly prohibited.

Why it matters

Prevents discriminatory pricing practices and ensures standard valuation across jurisdictions.

rule Verification Method

Cross-reference extracted MRP value against national product database via barcode correlation.
